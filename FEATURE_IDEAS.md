# 50 Feature Ideas for NOIR Launcher

A curated list of 50 high-impact features inspired by top minimalist and productivity launchers (Niagara, Before Launcher, Olauncher, Ratio by Blloc, Nothing OS, and Nova Launcher) designed to deliver an effortless, super-fast, and premium user experience.

---

### Quick Access & Favorites

- **Pinned Favorite Apps**: Pin 4 to 8 most-used applications at the very top of the home screen above the alphabetical list for instant one-tap access.
- **Swipe-Right Quick Actions**: Swipe right on any application row to trigger a custom secondary action (for example: swipe right on Camera to open QR Scanner, or Phone to call a favorite contact).
- **Embedded App Shortcuts**: Expand native Android app shortcuts (long-press shortcuts) directly under the app name on long press or swipe.
- **Dynamic Recent Apps Row**: An optional subtle top bar showing the last 3 to 5 opened applications that updates automatically throughout the day.
- **Time-Based Favorite Suggestions**: Dynamically surface relevant apps based on the time of day (for example: Spotify and Maps in the morning, Slack during work hours, YouTube in the evening).
- **Double-Tap Quick Launch**: Double-tap on empty screen space to immediately launch your most essential daily app (e.g., Camera or Notes).
- **Notification Dot / Count Preview**: Subtle graphite numeral or clean dot beside an app name showing unread notification count without needing to pull down the notification shade.

---

### Gestures & Shortcuts

- **Swipe Down Anywhere for Notification Shade**: Pull down anywhere on the home screen to expand the Android notification panel with zero reach required on tall screens.
- **Swipe Down with Two Fingers for Quick Settings**: Fast two-finger swipe down to open Android Quick Settings toggles (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Torch).
- **Swipe Up for Global Search**: Quick upward flick anywhere on the screen instantly focuses the search bar and opens the keyboard.
- **Double-Tap Screen to Lock**: Tap twice on empty paper space to turn off and lock the display using Android Accessibility Service.
- **Long-Press Wallpaper for Rapid Settings**: Press and hold anywhere on blank paper texture to quickly open Launcher Preferences or change themes.
- **Pinch In to Hide / Show Clutter**: Pinch inward to toggle "Zen Mode" (temporarily hide all text and apps, leaving pure tactile paper and time).
- **Edge Swipe Haptic Feedback Customization**: Granular slider in settings to adjust vibration intensity for alphabet scrubbing and swipe gestures.

---

### Smart Search & App Discovery

- **Direct Web & Calculator Search**: Type math expressions (e.g., "145 * 12") directly into the search bar for instant calculation results without opening a calculator app.
- **Direct Web Query Routing**: Press Enter or a subtle arrow in search to run the query directly in your default browser or search engine (Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave).
- **Contact Search & Direct Dialing**: Search for contact names directly in the search bar and tap to call or send a message immediately.
- **Fuzzy Search & Typo Tolerance**: Smarter search matching that tolerates minor typos (e.g., typing "spotfi" still finds "Spotify").
- **App Alias & Custom Nicknames**: Rename apps or assign custom search tags (e.g., tag "Instagram" with "social" or "insta" so searching either keyword finds it).
- **Search History & Recents**: Tap the search bar when empty to reveal the last 3 searched apps or queries for rapid re-launching.
- **Keyboard Auto-Show on Home**: An optional toggle to open the keyboard automatically whenever the home screen is displayed for ultra-fast launch by typing.

---

### Minimalist Widgets & Glanceable Info

- **Minimalist Weather Line**: Single-line editorial weather display beside the date (e.g., "Mon, Nov 2 · 22°C Clear").
- **Integrated Battery Indicator**: Subtle typographic battery percentage or thin horizontal graphite gauge integrated seamlessly into the header.
- **Next Calendar Event Snippet**: Display the next upcoming calendar meeting or reminder right under the date in clean Swiss typography.
- **Single Minimalist Widget Page**: Swipe left from home to a dedicated distraction-free page supporting 1 or 2 standard Android widgets (e.g., Notes, Audio Player).
- **Now Playing Music Controller**: Discreet editorial music ticker showing current track title and artist with tap-to-pause controls when music is playing.
- **Daily Step Counter Integration**: Optional health summary line displaying daily step count synced with Google Fit / Health Connect.
- **Custom Header Quote or Greeting**: Let users set a personalized motivational quote or daily mantra in serif typography at the top of their screen.

---

### Customization & Typography

- **Custom Font Loader (TTF / OTF)**: Allow users to import their own custom font files for home screen typography.
- **Font Size Scaling Slider**: Independent fine-grained font size adjustment for app titles, section headers, and the time display.
- **Hide App Labels Mode**: Option to display only icons in a grid or centered minimalist dock for users who prefer visual-only minimalism.
- **Text-Only Mode (Zero Icons)**: Option to hide all application icons completely, turning the home screen into a pure, clean typography-driven list.
- **Custom Paper Tint Color Picker**: Pick custom hex colors for the paper background (e.g., warm sepia, cool newsprint, matcha paper, slate gray).
- **Pure AMOLED Black Mode**: True `#000000` pitch black theme for maximum battery saving on OLED screens.
- **Third-Party Icon Pack Support**: Load standard icon packs downloaded from Google Play Store alongside Noir's built-in icon shape engine.

---

### Digital Wellbeing & Focus Mode

- **Daily App Screen Time Badges**: Subtle gray duration next to app names showing today's usage (e.g., "Instagram · 42m") to encourage mindful phone usage.
- **App Usage Pause / Friction Timer**: Add an intentional 5-second breathing countdown before opening addictive social media or gaming apps.
- **Focus / Work Profile Filter**: One-tap toggle to switch between "Work Mode" (work apps visible) and "Personal Mode" (work apps hidden).
- **Greyscale Display Mode Shortcut**: Fast toggle to activate system monochrome screen mode to make your phone instantly less stimulating.
- **App Renaming for Intention**: Allow users to rename distracting apps to remind them of their intent (e.g., rename YouTube to "Video Library").
- **Bedtime Auto-Dim Theme**: Automatically switch to dark charcoal mode and reduce contrast based on sunset or schedule.

---

### Organization & App Drawer

- **Custom App Folders / Categories**: Group apps into clean text-based expandable categories (e.g., "Finance", "Reading", "Tools").
- **App Tagging System**: Tag apps with multiple labels so they appear under multiple relevant searches or sections.
- **Batch App Hiding**: Multi-select interface in settings to quickly hide 20+ bloatware or utility apps in seconds.
- **Hide Apps Behind Biometric Lock**: Lock hidden or sensitive apps behind fingerprint or PIN authentication before they can be searched or opened.
- **Alphabet Scrubber Letter Filtering**: Tap a letter on the right-side curved scrubber to filter the list to only that letter's apps, rather than just scrolling.

---

### System, Backup & Power User Features

- **Settings Backup & Export to JSON**: Export all launcher preferences, custom tints, hidden apps, and pinned favorites to a single JSON backup file.
- **Settings Restore from File**: Easily import your saved configuration on a new phone or after a fresh install.
- **Direct APK Share from Context Menu**: Long-press an app to extract and share its installer APK directly via Bluetooth or messaging.
- **Zero Background Battery Consumption Optimization**: Strict sleep policy ensuring zero background CPU wake-locks when the screen is off or another app is open.
